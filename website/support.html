support

Questions, bugs, or feedback? Email support@marcyapp.com and we'll get back to you.

Notifications aren't firing

Reminders are available in the native iOS app, not the browser version. For the native app, check iOS Settings > Marcy > Notifications and make sure Allow Notifications is on.

Backing up or moving your data

Go to the data page and tap “export backup” to save your data as a file. If you reinstall the app or switch devices, use “import backup” to restore it. There is no account or cloud sync — the backup file is the only copy outside your device.

Deleting your data

Use “fresh start” on the Data page and confirm “erase data” to remove local history, settings, and recovery copies and cancel pending reminders. Downloaded backups are not deleted, and this does not remove an email address already submitted for product updates.

Predictions look off

Marcy starts with a 28-day default and uses eligible intervals between logged periods to estimate cycle length. You can also set it manually. Timing remains estimated, even with more history.

Something else

Email support@marcyapp.com with what happened and, if possible, a screenshot. We read every message.
