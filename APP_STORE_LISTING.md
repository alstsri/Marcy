## Description

Marcy tracks her cycle so you don't have to guess.

Know when PMS is coming. Understand why her mood shifted. Stop reacting and start anticipating. Marcy gives you a daily read on where she is in her cycle — what's happening hormonally, how it might affect her mood and energy, and how to navigate it.

Built for men who want to understand the patterns, make better decisions, and show up with intention.

WHAT MARCY DOES
• Tracks cycle phases — menstrual, follicular, ovulation, luteal, PMS
• Daily insights on what she's going through and how to connect
• Predicts fertile windows, ovulation, PMS, and next period
• Tracks tension patterns and correlates them to cycle phases
• Learns her rhythm over time with weighted cycle calculations
• Reassurance when cycles vary — because they do

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• All data stays on your device — nothing is sent to any server
• No account required
• No login, no cloud, no tracking
• Export and import your data anytime

Marcy is not a medical device. All predictions are estimates based on general medical conventions. Consult a healthcare provider for medical decisions.

### Privacy Policy for Marcy

**Last updated: April 2026**

**Summary: Marcy stores all data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store your health data.**

#### Data Storage
All cycle tracking data — including period dates, tension events, and settings — is stored exclusively in your device's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

#### Email Collection
If you voluntarily provide your email address, it is submitted to Formspree (formspree.io), a third-party form handling service, for the sole purpose of sending product updates. Your email is not linked to your health data in any way. You may request removal of your email by contacting us.

#### Health Data
Marcy does not integrate with Apple Health, HealthKit, or any other health data framework. No health data is collected, transmitted, or shared.

#### Analytics and Tracking
Marcy does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, cookies, or any form of user tracking.

#### Data Deletion
Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by deleting the app. No server-side data exists to delete, with the exception of voluntarily submitted email addresses.

#### Third-Party Services
The only third-party service used is Formspree (formspree.io) for optional email collection. No other third-party services receive any data from the app.

#### Changes to This Policy
We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

#### Contact
For questions about this privacy policy, contact: themarcyapp@gmail.com
