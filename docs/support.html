marcy — support

Questions, bugs, or feedback? Email themarcyapp@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

Notifications aren't firing

Marcy needs notification permission to alert you about fertile windows, PMS, and period predictions. Check iOS Settings > Marcy > Notifications and make sure Allow Notifications is on.

Backing up or moving your data

Go to the data page and tap "export backup" to save your data as a file. If you reinstall the app or switch devices, use "import backup" to restore it. There is no account or cloud sync — the backup file is the only copy outside your device.

Deleting your data

All data lives on your device only. Deleting individual period or tension entries can be done from the data page. Deleting the app removes everything.

Predictions look off

Marcy starts with a 28-day cycle assumption and refines its predictions as you log more periods. Accuracy improves after your second and third logged cycle.
